\section*{Overview}
This document provides supplementary tables and technical specifications supporting the manuscript submitted to \textit{Data Technologies and Applications} (Emerald Publishing). The contents include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Table~S1:} Comprehensive literature positioning comparing selected federated recommendation, privacy, and explainability studies.
    \item \textbf{Table~S2:} Complete system hyperparameter specifications across model architecture, federated learning, differential privacy, homomorphic encryption, and post-hoc explainers.
    \item \textbf{Table~S3:} Cross-dataset validation comparing empirical ranking, latency, and explanation sensitivity metrics between the proprietary enterprise e-commerce benchmark (EARS) and the public movie benchmark (MovieLens-100k).
\end{itemize}
